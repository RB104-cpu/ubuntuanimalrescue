About Ubuntu Animal Rescue

Compassionate care for animals since 2010

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Ubuntu Animal Rescue began with a simple mission: to provide shelter, medical care, and loving homes for abandoned and abused animals across South Africa. The name "Ubuntu" reflects our philosophy - "I am because we are" - emphasizing community and interconnectedness.

What We Do

  • Rescue abandoned and abused animals
  • Provide medical treatment and rehabilitation
  • Facilitate adoptions to loving homes
  • Educate communities about animal welfare
  • Run spay/neuter programs
