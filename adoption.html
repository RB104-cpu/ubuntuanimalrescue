Adoption FAQ

What are the adoption fees?

Adoption fees range from R500 to R1500 depending on the animal. This includes vaccinations, spay/neuter, and microchipping.

What are the requirements to adopt?

You must be 18+ years old, have valid ID, proof of address, and consent from all household members.

Can I return an animal if it doesn't work out?

Yes, we have a 30-day trial period. If the adoption doesn't work out, we'll take the animal back and help you find a better match.
